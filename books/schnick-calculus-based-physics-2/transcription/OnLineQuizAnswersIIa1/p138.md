## Question 1

0 of 20 points

Given the expression below valid for points in the x-y plane, what is the y-component of the electric field valid for points on the y-axis?

$$V = \frac {k Q}{L} \ln \left[ \frac {L - x + \sqrt {(L - x) ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}}{- x + \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}} \right]$$

Selected Answer: ✗

$$- \frac {k Q}{L y}$$

Correct Answer: ✓

$$\frac {k Q}{L} \left[ \frac {1}{y} - \frac {y}{L ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + L \sqrt {L ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}} \right]$$
