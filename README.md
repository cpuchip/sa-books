# sa-books

Math and physics books under share-alike licences (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike), kept as
acquired, each with its attribution and its licence. This is the sibling of
[pd-books](https://github.com/cpuchip/pd-books), which holds public-domain books under CC0. A book
whose licence asks for attribution and share-alike lives here instead, so its terms travel with it.

The layout follows pd-books:

    books/<slug>/source/       the files exactly as acquired, with SHA256SUMS
    books/<slug>/book.json     title, author, edition, licence, source files and URLs
    books/<slug>/found.json    errata we found ourselves, and candidates, each with its evidence
    books/<slug>/evidence/     what settled an entry
    export/                    the books as data for books.cpuchip.net and learn.cpuchip.net

No book is here yet.

## The books

### Calculus-Based Physics, by Jeffrey W. Schnick

Calculus-Based Physics II (the second volume; the first is not acquired yet), by Jeffrey W. Schnick
(Saint Anselm College), from [cbphysics.org](http://www.cbphysics.org/). The book's own statement: "Copyright 2006, Jeffrey W.
Schnick, Creative Commons Attribution Share-Alike License 2.5."

Attribution follows the author's
[Specification of Manner of Attribution](http://www.cbphysics.org/howtoAttribute.html):

- An excerpt carries, on the same page: "online textbook Calculus-Based Physics by Jeffrey Schnick."
- A modified release is titled "Calculus-Based Physics by Jeffrey Schnick as modified by" the
  modifier's name.

## Licence

- Each book's source files are the author's, under the licence the book states (for Calculus-Based
  Physics, CC BY-SA 2.5).
- What we derive from a book (transcriptions, problem records, answers, errata, the export) is
  released under the same licence as the book, as share-alike requires: for Calculus-Based Physics,
  [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/), whose legal code is in
  `LICENSE-CC-BY-SA-2.5`.
