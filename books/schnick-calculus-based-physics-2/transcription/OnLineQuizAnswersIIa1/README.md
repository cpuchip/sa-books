# On-line quiz answers, second semester (with handwritten worked solutions)

Calculus-Based Physics by Jeffrey Schnick as modified by Michael Stufflebeam.

Transcription of Calculus-Based Physics II, its online quizzes and its SAC problems, by Jeffrey W. Schnick (http://www.cbphysics.org/). Licensed under CC BY-SA 2.5 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/).

One file per page of `OnLineQuizAnswersIIa1.pdf`, `p001.md` to `p153.md`, each with its source page and how it was read in its front matter. Figures are cropped from the page images into `fig/`. Not reproduced: the running heads and page numbers, and Blackboard's own interface in the quiz screenshots (icons, buttons, dropdowns). Italics are kept only on the pages that were proofread against their images.
