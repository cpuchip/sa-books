Question 2 0 of 20 points

Two perfectly conducting spheres are in an evacuated region of space. One is at a potential of $-12$ volts and the other is at a potential of $15$ volts. A free electron is released from rest at the surface of the sphere at $-12$ volts. With what speed does it crash into the other sphere? (The mass of an electron is $9.11 \times 10^{-31}$ kg.)

Selected Answer: ✗ 0 m/s

Correct Answer: ✓ $3.1 \times 10^{6} \text{ m/s}$

Feedback: This is a conservation of energy problem. Sought is the speed of the electron at the instant before it hits the 15 volt sphere.
