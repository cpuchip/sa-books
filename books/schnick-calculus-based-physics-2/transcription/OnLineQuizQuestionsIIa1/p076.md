## Question 1

## 20 points

What is linear charge density? (Choose the one BEST answer.)

It is the charge density that lies along a line.

It is the total charge of an object divided by the length of that object.

In the case of a distribution of charge in which all the charge is on a line, it is the total amount of charge on that line divided by the length of the line.

In the case of a distribution of charge in which all the charge is on a line, it is the charge-per-length characterizing any element of the line (with units of Coulombs per meter).
