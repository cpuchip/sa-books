Because the coil becomes closer and closer to the magnet, the number of magnetic field lines through the coil is increasing with time, and, as a result of this changing magnetic-field-through-the-coil, there is indeed a current induced to flow in the coil.

## Question 3

0 of 20 points

A long straight piece of wire carries an increasing current due northward. Due east of, and very near the straight wire, at the same height as that of the straight wire, is a stationary horizontal loop of wire. What is the direction of the current, if any, induced to flow in the loop?

Selected Answer: ✗ Northward.

Correct Answer: ✓ Counterclockwise as viewed from above.

Feedback: Look at the straight wire by itself, from the south. In this view, toward the top of the page is up toward the sky, not northward.
