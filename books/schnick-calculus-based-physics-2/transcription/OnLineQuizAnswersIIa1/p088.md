## Question 5

12 of 12 points

Light can travel through vacuum.

Selected Answer: ✓ True

Correct Answer: ✓ True

Feedback: Nice job.

In classical physics, light (electromagnetic radiation) is the only kind of wave that does not need a medium. For sound waves in air, the air is the medium. It is the "air" that is waving. In the case of light, it is the electric and magnetic fields that do the waving, and they are the light itself.
