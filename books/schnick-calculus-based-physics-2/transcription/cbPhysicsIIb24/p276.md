Copying that equation here:

$$
\int dE_{x} = \int_{-0.180\,\mathrm{m}}^{+0.180\,\mathrm{m}} \left(0.00120\,\frac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{m}^{3}}\right) \frac{k\, y'^{2}\, x\, dy'}{\left(x^{2}+y'^{2}\right)^{3/2}}
$$

we note that on the left is the infinite sum of all the contributions to the x component of the electric field due to all the infinitesimal elements of the line of charge. We don't need any special mathematics techniques to evaluate that. The sum of all the parts is the whole. That is, on the left, we have $E_{x}$.

The right side, we can evaluate. First, let's factor out the constants:

$$
E_{x} = \left(0.00120\,\frac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{m}^{3}}\right) k\, x \int_{-0.180\,\mathrm{m}}^{+0.180\,\mathrm{m}} \frac{y'^{2}\, dy'}{\left(x^{2}+y'^{2}\right)^{3/2}}
$$

The integral is given on your formula sheet. Carrying out the integration yields:

$$
E_{x} = \left(0.00120\,\frac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{m}^{3}}\right) k\, x \left[ \frac{y'}{\sqrt{x^{2}+y'^{2}}} + \ln\left(y' + \sqrt{x^{2}+y'^{2}}\right) \right]_{-0.180\,\mathrm{m}}^{+0.180\,\mathrm{m}}
$$

$$
E_{x} = \left(.00120\,\frac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{m}^{3}}\right) k\, x \cdot \left\{ \left[ \frac{+.180\,\mathrm{m}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+(+.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}}} + \ln\left(+.180\,\mathrm{m} + \sqrt{x^{2}+(+.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}}\right) \right] - \right.
$$

$$
\left. \left[ \frac{-.180\,\mathrm{m}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+(-.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}}} + \ln\left(-.180\,\mathrm{m} + \sqrt{x^{2}+(-.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}}\right) \right] \right\}
$$

$$
E_{x} = \left(.00120\,\frac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{m}^{3}}\right) k\, x \cdot \left[ \frac{.360\,\mathrm{m}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}}} + \ln \frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}} + .180\,\mathrm{m}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}} - .180\,\mathrm{m}} \right]
$$

Substituting the value of the Coulomb constant $k$ from the formula sheet we obtain

$$
E_{x} = \left(.00120\,\frac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{m}^{3}}\right) 8.99 \times 10^{9}\,\frac{\mathrm{N}\cdot\mathrm{m}^{2}}{\mathrm{C}^{2}}\, x \cdot \left[ \frac{.360\,\mathrm{m}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}}} + \ln \frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}} + .180\,\mathrm{m}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}} - .180\,\mathrm{m}} \right]
$$

Finally we have

$$
E_{x} = 1.08 \times 10^{7}\,\frac{\mathrm{N}}{\mathrm{C}\cdot\mathrm{m}}\, x \cdot \left[ \frac{.360\,\mathrm{m}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}}} + \ln \frac{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}} + .180\,\mathrm{m}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+(.180\,\mathrm{m})^{2}} - .180\,\mathrm{m}} \right]
$$
