## Question 1

Given the electric potential, as a function of the Cartesian coordinates \( x, y, \) and \( z \); how does one determine the electric field?

○ By means of a single integration.

○ Algebraically.

○ The x-component of the electric field is determined by taking the negative of the derivative of the electric potential with respect to x. The y-component of the electric field is determined by taking the negative of derivative of the electric potential with respect to y.
