## Selected Answer:

✗ Because the resistors are in series, the whole ten volts goes through each resistor.

Correct Answer: ✓ Voltage is potential difference. It doesn't ever go through anything. It doesn't "go" anywhere. It exists.

Feedback: What does "go" in a circuit, is charge. Charge flows through a circuit. The flow rate of charge is called current.

Voltage exists. Current flows.

(The statement that "current flows" is jargon. Technically it is charge that flows and current is the charge flow rate. But the expression "current flows" has pervaded the field so it will not be avoided.)
