What is the defining effect of a magnetic field?

## Selected

✗ It exerts a force on a charged particle at rest in the magnetic field.

## Answer:

## Correct

√ It exerts a torque on a magnetic dipole (bar magnet) that is in the magnetic field as long as the magnetic dipole is not aligned with the magnetic field.

## Answer:

## Feedback:

A magnetic field is an invisible entity that exists in a region of space that may be void of all matter, in the vicinity of a permanent magnet or an electric current. The magnetic field is a vector field, an infinite set of vectors, one at each point in the region of space where the magnetic field exists. The cause of a magnetic field is either a permanent magnet or an electric current. The defining effect of a magnetic field is to exert a torque on a magnetic dipole that is in the magnetic field, as long as the magnetic dipole is not parallel or anti-parallel to the magnetic field.
