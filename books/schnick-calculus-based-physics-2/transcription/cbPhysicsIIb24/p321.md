Using $\int \frac{dx}{(x^2 + a^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}} = \frac{1}{a^2}\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + a^2}}$ from your formula sheet; and; identifying $x^{2} + y^{2}$ as $a^2$, and, $u$ as the $x$ appearing on the formula sheet, we obtain:

$$
B _ {x} = - \frac {\mu_ {\mathrm{o}} I}{4 \pi} y \frac {1}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} \frac {u}{\sqrt {u ^ {2} + x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}} \Bigg | _ {- \infty} ^ {\infty}
$$

Now, I need to take the limit of that expression as $ u $ goes to $ \infty $ and again as $ u $ goes to $ -\infty $. To facilitate that, I want to factor a $ u $ out of the square root in the denominator. But, I have to be careful. The expression $ \sqrt{u^2 + x^2 + y^2} $, which is equivalent to $ \sqrt{(z - z')^2 + x^2 + y^2} $ is a distance. That means it is inherently positive, whether $ u $ (or $ z' $ for that matter) is positive or negative. So, when I factor $ u $ out of the square root, I'm going to have to use absolute value signs. For the denominator: $ \sqrt{u^2 + x^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{u^2 \left(1 + \frac{x^2}{u^2} + \frac{y^2}{u^2}\right)} = |u| \sqrt{1 + \frac{x^2}{u^2} + \frac{y^2}{u^2}} $, so,

$$
B _ {x} = - \frac {\mu_ {\mathrm{o}} I}{4 \pi} y \frac {1}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} \frac {u}{| u |} \frac {1}{\sqrt {1 + \frac {x ^ {2}}{u ^ {2}} + \frac {y ^ {2}}{u ^ {2}}}} \Bigg | _ {- \infty} ^ {\infty}
$$

$$
B _ {x} = - \frac {\mu_ {\mathrm{o}} I}{4 \pi} y \frac {1}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} \left(1 \frac {1}{\sqrt {1 + 0 + 0}} - - 1 \frac {1}{\sqrt {1 + 0 + 0}}\right)
$$

$$
B _ {x} = - \frac {\mu_ {\mathrm{o}} I}{4 \pi} y \frac {1}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} (2)
$$

$$
B _ {x} = - \frac {\mu_ {\mathrm{o}} I}{2 \pi} y \frac {1}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}
$$

Now for the y component. Recall that we had:

$$
\mathbf {d} \overrightarrow {\mathbf {B}} = - \frac {\mu_ {\mathrm{o}} I}{4 \pi} y \frac {d z ^ {\prime}}{\left[ x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + \left(z - z ^ {\prime}\right) ^ {2} \right] ^ {3 / 2}} \hat {\mathbf {I}} + \frac {\mu_ {\mathrm{o}} I}{4 \pi} x \frac {d z ^ {\prime}}{\left[ x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + \left(z - z ^ {\prime}\right) ^ {2} \right] ^ {3 / 2}} \hat {\mathbf {J}}
$$

so,

$$
d B _ {y} = \frac {\mu_ {\mathrm{o}} I}{4 \pi} x \frac {d z ^ {\prime}}{\left[ x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + (z - z ^ {\prime}) ^ {2} \right] ^ {3 / 2}}
$$
