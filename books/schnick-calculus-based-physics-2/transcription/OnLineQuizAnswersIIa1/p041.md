## Question 1

## 0 of 15 points

What all can the power P possibly represent in the expression P = IV where I is current and V is voltage?

Selected The rate at which energy is delivered to a resistor by the rest of a circuit of which that resistor is a part.

Correct The rate at which energy is delivered to a resistor by the rest of a circuit of which that resistor is a part. Answers:

√ The rate at which energy is delivered to a circuit by a seat of EMF.

√ The rate at which electrical energy is converted to thermal energy in a circuit.

√ The rate at which heat is given off by a resistor when the voltage across the resistor is, and has for a long time been, V and the current through the resistor is, and has for a long time been, I.

√ The rate at which chemical energy is converted to electrical energy in a battery that is in a circuit.

Feedback: The short definition for power is "the rate at which work is done." This definition encompasses all the responses for this question.

## Question 2

## 0 of 10 points

What are some of the differences between resistance and resistivity?

Selected
Answers: ✓ Resistance can be used to characterize a given piece of wire whereas resistivity characterizes the material of which the wire is made.

Correct
Answers:
✓ Resistance can be used to characterize a given piece of wire whereas resistivity characterizes the material of which the wire is made.
✓ Resistance is measured in ohms whereas resistivity is measured in ohm's per meter.
