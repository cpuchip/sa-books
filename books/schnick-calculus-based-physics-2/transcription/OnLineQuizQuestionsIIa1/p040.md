## Question 1

## 20 points

Indicate all that is wrong about the statement, "The magnetic field lines of a stationary bar magnet move from the north pole of the bar magnet to the south pole of the bar magnet."

There is no such thing as a bar magnet.

The magnetic field lines do not "move" anywhere. They exist. They do have some extent in space so we do say that they extend from here to there but it is incorrect to say that they move from here to there. One should not say that they "go" from here to there either.

Outside the bar magnet the magnetic field lines do extend from north pole to south pole, but, inside the bar magnet, the magnetic field lines extend from south pole to north pole. Without stating the location of the magnetic field lines being characterized, the given statement is ambiguous.

Magnets do not have north and south poles, they have positive and negative ends.
