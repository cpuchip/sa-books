## Question 2

## 20 points

What are the angles between the direction of travel of light, the electric field, and the associated magnetic field?

They are all in the same direction, hence the angles between them are all zero.

The angle between the direction of travel and either field is always 90 degrees, but the angle between the electric field and the magnetic field can have any value.

They are each at an angle of 90 degrees with respect to each of the other two.

All the angles are arbitrary.
