## SAC404

1) Two particles are at fixed positions in space. Their positions are specified in terms of a Cartesian coordinate system as follows:

One particle, of charge -84 nC (recall nC is to be read "nanocoulombs" and means $\times 10^{-9}\ \text{C}$), is at the origin. The other particle has charge +45 nC and its position vector is $4.0\ \text{cm}\ \hat{\imath} + 5.0\ \text{cm}\ \hat{k}$.

a) Find the electric field vector, expressed in unit vector notation, at the point whose position vector is $2.0\ \text{cm}\ \hat{\imath} + 3.0\ \text{cm}\ \hat{\jmath}$.

b) Find the force that would act on each of the following particles if it were placed at the position at which you found the electric field in part a:

i) an electron,

ii) a doubly ionized Helium Ion He$^{++}$ (charge = +2e),

iii) a particle whose charge is +.055 nC.
