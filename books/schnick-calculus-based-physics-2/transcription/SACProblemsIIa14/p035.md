## SAC401

1) The position vector, with respect to the origin of a Cartesian coordinate system, of a particle with charge $0.640\ \mu\text{C}$ is $1.30\ \text{cm}\ \hat{\imath} + 1.95\ \text{cm}\ \hat{\jmath} - 1.00\ \text{cm}\ \hat{k}$. The position vector of a second particle, this one of charge $0.450\ \mu\text{C}$, is $1.88\ \text{cm}\ \hat{\imath} - 1.05\ \text{cm}\ \hat{\jmath} + 1.40\ \text{cm}\ \hat{k}$. Find the force exerted on the second particle by the first.

Hint: Force is a vector. Because vectors given in the problem are specified in unit vector notation, the answer should be specified in unit vector notation.
