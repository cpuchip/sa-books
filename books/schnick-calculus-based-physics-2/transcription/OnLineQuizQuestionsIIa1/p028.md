A seat of EMF is in series with a resistor  $R_{1}$  and with a parallel combination of two other resistors  $R_{2}$  and  $R_{3}$ .  $R_{2}$  is a variable resistor whose resistance can be changed just by turning a knob. As the knob is turned to increase the resistance of  $R_{2}$  what happens to the voltage across  $R_{3}$ ?

It increases.

It decreases.

It stays the same.
