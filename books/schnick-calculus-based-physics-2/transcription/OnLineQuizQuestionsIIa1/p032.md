## 20 points

Consider a capacitor which is charged to 12 volts and then connected across a resistor. Let  $t_{1/2}$  be the time it takes for the voltage to drop down to 6 volts. Suppose the experiment is repeated, but with a pair of capacitors connected in series in place of the original capacitor, where each element of the pair has the same capacitance as the original capacitor. How does the new  $t_{1/2}$  compare with the original  $t_{1/2}$ ?
