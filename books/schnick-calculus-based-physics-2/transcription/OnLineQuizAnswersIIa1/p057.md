$$V = V_o e^{-t/\tau}$$

At $t = t_{1/2}$ $V = \frac{1}{2} V_o$

so:

$$\frac{1}{2} V_o = V_o e^{-t_{1/2}/\tau}$$

$$\frac{1}{2} = e^{-t_{1/2}/\tau}$$

$$\ln \frac{1}{2} = -\frac{t_{1/2}}{\tau}$$

$$\frac{t_{1/2}}{\tau} = -\ln \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\frac{t_{1/2}}{\tau} = \ln 2$$

$$t_{\frac{1}{2}} = (\ln 2)\,\tau$$

but $\tau = RC$

$$t_{1/2} = (\ln 2)\, RC$$

Replacing $C$ with 2 identical capacitors $C$ in series is the same as replacing $C$ with $C_s = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{C} + \frac{1}{C}} = \frac{1}{2} C$.

$$t'_{1/2} = (\ln 2)\, R C_s$$

$$= (\ln 2)\, R\left(\tfrac{1}{2} C\right)$$

$$= \tfrac{1}{2} (\ln 2)\, RC$$

$$\boxed{t'_{1/2} = \tfrac{1}{2}\, t_{1/2}}$$
