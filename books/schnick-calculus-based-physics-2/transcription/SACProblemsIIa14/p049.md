## SAC435

## Biot Savart

1) A wire segment carries a current I along the y axis from y = L to the origin. Find the magnetic field, due to this current-carrying wire segment, at  $(L, L)$ .

2) A wire segment carries a current I along the x axis from the origin to infinity. Find an expression for the magnetic field, due to the current-carrying wire, on the y axis valid for points at which y > 0.
