$$\mathbf{d}\vec{\mathbf{B}} = \frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} \frac{dz' \hat{k} \times \left(x \hat{\imath} + y \hat{\jmath} + (z - z') \hat{k}\right)}{\left[x^2 + y^2 + (z - z')^2\right]^{3/2}}$$

$$\mathbf{d}\vec{\mathbf{B}} = \frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} \frac{dz' \left(x \hat{k} \times \hat{\imath} + y \hat{k} \times \hat{\jmath} + (z - z') \hat{k} \times \hat{k}\right)}{\left[x^2 + y^2 + (z - z')^2\right]^{3/2}}$$

$$\mathbf{d}\vec{\mathbf{B}} = \frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} \frac{dz' \left(x \hat{\jmath} - y \hat{\imath}\right)}{\left[x^2 + y^2 + (z - z')^2\right]^{3/2}}$$

$$\mathbf{d}\vec{\mathbf{B}} = -\frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} y \frac{dz'}{\left[x^2 + y^2 + (z - z')^2\right]^{3/2}} \hat{\imath} + \frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} x \frac{dz'}{\left[x^2 + y^2 + (z - z')^2\right]^{3/2}} \hat{\jmath}$$

Let's work on this a component at a time. For the x component, we have:

$$dB_x = -\frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} y \frac{dz'}{\left[x^2 + y^2 + (z - z')^2\right]^{3/2}}$$

Integrating over $z'$ from $-\infty$ to $\infty$ yields:

$$B_x = -\frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} y \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{dz'}{\left[x^2 + y^2 + (z - z')^2\right]^{3/2}}$$

I'm going to go with the following variable substitution:

$$u = z - z'$$

$$du = -dz', \text{ so, } dz' = -du$$

Upper Limit: Evaluating $u = z - z'$ at $z' = \infty$ yields $-\infty$ for the upper limit of integration.

Lower Limit: Evaluating $u = z - z'$ at $z' = -\infty$ yields $\infty$ for the lower limit of integration.

So, our integral becomes:

$$B_x = -\frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} y \int_{\infty}^{-\infty} \frac{-du}{\left(x^2 + y^2 + u^2\right)^{3/2}}$$

I choose to use one of the minus signs to interchange the limits of integration:

$$B_x = -\frac{\mu_{\mathrm{o}} I}{4\pi} y \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{du}{\left(x^2 + y^2 + u^2\right)^{3/2}}$$
