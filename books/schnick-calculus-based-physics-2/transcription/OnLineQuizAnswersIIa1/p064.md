$$C_2 = C$$

so

$$C_s = 1/(1/C + 1/C)$$

$$C_s = 1/(2/C)$$

$$C_s = C/2.$$

The effective capacitance of a pair of capacitors in parallel, $C_p$, is given by

$$C_p = C_1 + C_2$$

For the case of two capacitors with one and the same capacitance C:

$$C_1 = C$$

and

$$C_2 = C$$

so

$$C_p = C + C$$

$$C_p = 2C.$$

Since $C_s = C/2$ and $C_p = 2C$ we have $C_p > C_s$. That is to say that the capacitance of the pair of identical capacitors is greater when the capacitors are connected in parallel.
