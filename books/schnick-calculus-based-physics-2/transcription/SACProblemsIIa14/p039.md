## SAC419

1) The area of a horizontal wire loop depends on time t as:  $A = 0.850 \, m^{2} e^{-125 s^{-1} t}$  (where m and s are the units meters and seconds respectively). The loop is, at all times, in a horizontal plane in a region of space where there exists a constant uniform upward-directed 0.505 T magnetic field. Find the EMF in the loop at t = .100 s.
