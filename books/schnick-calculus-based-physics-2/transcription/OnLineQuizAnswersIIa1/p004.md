## Question 10

## 0 of 8 points

Consider two charged particles. At a given separation each particle exerts a force of .12 N on the other. What force does each exert on the other after someone moves the particles closer together so that their distance is half the original distance?

Selected Answer: ✗ .03 N

Correct Answer: ✓ .48 N

Feedback: The force exerted on one charged particle by another is proportional to the reciprocal of the square of the separation of the two charged particles. So, if the separation is changed to  $\frac{1}{2}$  of the original separation then the force becomes the reciprocal of the square of  $\frac{1}{2}$ , times the original force. The reciprocal of the square of  $\frac{1}{2}$  is 4. So the new force is 4 times the old force. The old force was .12 N so the new force must be .48 N.
