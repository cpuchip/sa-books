In applying Kirchhoff's voltage law, one draws a schematic diagram of the circuit and then indicates the current in each leg (a.k.a. branch) of the circuit. For each leg of the circuit, how does one know which way to draw the current.

It doesn't matter which way one draws the current. One should just make a quick guess.

One always knows the high (electric potential) terminal of each circuit element. The current always goes through a circuit element from the high side of the circuit element to the low side. One should draw the current arrows so as to depict the current going into the high side and out the low side of every circuit element.

The current directions must always be given in any circuits problem. One should depict the currents to all be flowing in the given direction.
